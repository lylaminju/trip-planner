# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Public visitors are people evaluating whether Plotinerary is worth requesting
access to before the service has public signup. They need to understand the
planning workflow quickly, without reading a long product tour. Existing
invite-only users also need a clear path to sign in.

## Product Purpose

Plotinerary helps people turn saved places into a dated itinerary that is easy
to understand in both text and map form. Its core value is a single planning
surface where timeline items, map markers, and routes stay connected so a trip
can be planned and reviewed at a glance.

The production service name is Plotinerary. The name is documented in
`docs/decisions/2026-06-08-service-name-plotinerary.md`.

## Brand Personality

Straightforward, map-native, glanceable.

The interface should feel direct and practical. It should make the relationship
between Google Maps data, itinerary timing, and visual routes feel integrated
rather than bolted on.

## Anti-references

Avoid common AI-generated landing-page patterns: verbose marketing copy, generic
card grids, decorative gradients, placeholder illustrations, repetitive section
eyebrows, and visuals that distract from the planning workflow. Avoid pages that
require visitors to study the product before understanding the core promise.

## Design Principles

- Show the one-page planner workflow before explaining it.
- Keep copy short enough that visitors can scan the page quickly.
- Treat map and itinerary as one connected object, not two separate features.
- Make access status clear: invite-only today, request access for new visitors,
  sign in for existing users.
- Preserve a pragmatic product feel even on brand-facing pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction standards. Preserve keyboard access,
visible focus states, semantic structure, and reduced-motion alternatives for
any animation.
